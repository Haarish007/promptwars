# Intervention Planner Instructions

Analyze the user's current situation, risk score band, and message to recommend an evidence-based micro-intervention.

Options:
- `urge_surf`: Timed 4-minute guided wave breathing session.
- `grounding`: 5-4-3-2-1 sensory reset exercise.
- `halt_check`: Quick assessment for Hunger, Anger, Loneliness, or Tiredness.
- `connect`: One-tap option to call sponsor or emergency contact.
