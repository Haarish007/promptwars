# Crisis Support & Resources

I hear that you are going through an extraordinarily difficult moment right now. Your safety and well-being are what matter most.

Please connect with human support immediately. You do not have to carry this alone.

### Immediate Actions:
- Call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline - free, confidential, 24/7)
- Contact your emergency contact or sponsor using the one-tap buttons below
- If you are in immediate medical danger, please call emergency services (911) right away.
